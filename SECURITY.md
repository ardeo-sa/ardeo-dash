# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please use **GitHub Security Advisories** for private disclosure:

- Open the repository’s **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability**.

If Advisories is unavailable, email: gitsecurity@ardeo.com
(Include a minimal, reproducible description and potential impact.)

## Scope

- We actively maintain the `main` branch.

## Our commitment

- Acknowledge within **3 business days**.
- Provide a remediation plan or fix within **90 days**, sooner for critical issues.

## Out of scope (examples)

- Social engineering, physical attacks, or volumetric DoS.
- Findings without demonstrable security impact.
